What was tried and did not hold is part of the evidence on which the
main results rest, together with the criteria sealed before scoring: a
reader can check which alternatives were tested and closed before the
method was settled. Each entry gives its \emph{stored verdict}, the
outcome written in the project's report of that run, which we copy
rather than recompute. The
ledger is an extract. It keeps only the entries that bear directly on
the line of work from REL\_GLOBAL to CD reported in this thesis, and it
is not a complete record of every experiment in the PROPHET project.
Table~\ref{tab:rej-hypotheses} lists the hypotheses of the diagnostic
phase (Chapter~\ref{ch:diagnosis}) whose outcome can be traced to a
stored report or to a figure in this thesis,
Table~\ref{tab:rej-candidates} the method candidates and their stored
verdicts, and Table~\ref{tab:rej-withdrawn} the claims that we withdrew
during the analysis. Names in typewriter type are the identifiers under
which the runs were stored. The three rejections that matter most to the
argument are discussed in Section~\ref{sec:disc-rejected}; the first
table keeps them as one line each so that the ledger is complete.

\section{Method candidates and their stored verdicts}
\label{sec:rej-candidates}

The first fifteen rows of Table~\ref{tab:rej-candidates} are the
stored verdicts of the method candidates that led to CD, and each was
judged against its own control under a criterion written before
scoring. These rows are a record of the research history, judged each
against its own control, not a basis for methodological comparison
across rows. Where the stored contract or report states what a
candidate changed and what it was compared with, the table gives a
brief definition after its stored identifier; the full inputs,
objectives, controls and settings remain in those archived contracts
and reports.

The last five rows have a
different status. They are post hoc development analyses on runs whose
scores had already been seen, and no new model was trained for the two
extension candidates. Candidate~A replaces the teacher mean by a target
that falls back toward $\pi$, the boundary-weighted FIT action
frequencies, when few teachers agree.
Candidate~B keeps only the directions of the hidden state that the 30
seeds share on FIT inputs. Like the adaptive ensemble distillation of
Du et al.~\cite{du2020agree}, Candidate~A responds to disagreement among
teachers, but it does so by interpolating the probability target toward
$\pi$ rather than by weighting teachers in gradient space.
% 원문: Du et al. NeurIPS 2020 초록 "a tolerance parameter to accommodate disagreement among teachers",
%   "a dynamic weighting method for each teacher" (§3.1 gradient 공간 다목적 최적화). 확인됨.
It also
differs from
ensemble distribution distillation~\cite{malinin2019ensemble}, which keeps the
distribution of the ensemble's predictions rather than falling back to
fixed frequencies.
% 원문: Malinin et al. arXiv:1905.00076 초록 "distilling the distribution of the predictions from an
%   ensemble, rather than just the average prediction". 확인됨.
Candidate~B builds on comparing networks through a shared
representation subspace~\cite{raghu2017svcca}.
% 원문: Raghu et al. NIPS 2017 초록 "comparing two representations ... invariant to affine transform"; §2
%   SVD 뒤 CCA 로 두 층 부분공간 정렬. 확인됨.
Candidate~A scored better than the teacher mean on ADS-24
but worse in-house. Candidate~B narrowed the spread on the unseen
condition of ADS-24 but raised the loss on its preservation runs from 1.449 to
1.882. Neither was carried forward to a fit.

\section{Claims withdrawn during the analysis}
\label{sec:rej-withdrawn}

The withdrawn claims in Table~\ref{tab:rej-withdrawn} were not
rejected by a test. They were errors of reading, arithmetic, or design
that we found and corrected. The last two are discussed in Section~\ref{sec:disc-rejected};
the 10-seed gate is also why every later contract made the seed standard
deviation the primary measure (Section~\ref{sec:results-failed}).

  & Withdrawn \\

  & From v2 on, the seed standard deviation is the primary measure \\

Three calculations made while we examined a proposed corrector for
receipt delay were also corrected; they are not hypotheses and are not
in the tables. The proposal multiplied the baseline distribution $p_0$
by $A(x) = \rho_0 I + \rho_1 T + \rho_2 T^2$, a polynomial in a
transition term $T$, and was never adopted. First, starting from
$\rho_0 \approx 0.999$ was described as guaranteeing preservation; it
only keeps the initial output, and keeping the $+0.020$ limit after
training requires the constraint $\rho_0 \ge e^{-0.020} \approx 0.980$.
Second, a resolution argument held that a one-row loss of about 0.03
made a difference of 0.013 undetectable; it omitted the mean over
seeds: a change of 10 in the loss of one row of one seed contributes
only $10/(5 \times 341) = 0.005865$ to the mean over five seeds and the
341 rows of \texttt{C\_ONLY}. Third, a maximum score difference of
about 10.3 was given for the 18 logits under LogitNorm, whose logit
vector has norm $1/\tau = 10$. The bound on the difference between any
two of the 18 scores is $|z_i - z_j| \le \sqrt{2}\,\lVert z \rVert =
10\sqrt{2} \approx 14.14$; 10.3 is the value in the special arrangement
in which the other 17 scores are equal.
% 저자 답(정의 묶음 18(a)): 세 계산은 수신 지연 보정기 제안을 검토하던 분석 단계의 정정이라 표에서 빼고
%   이 문단으로 옮겼다. 10.3 검산: a²+17b²=100, b=−a/17 → a−b = 10·√(18/17)... = 10.29.
%   |z_i−z_j| ≤ √2‖z‖ (‖z‖=10)은 코시–슈바르츠에서 나온다. 341 = C_ONLY 행 수(저자 답의 5×341).
%   T의 정체(전이 행렬인지)는 저자 답에 없어 "transition term"으로만 썼다.

% 이 부록의 질문은 결론 뒤 "저자 확인 목록"에 모았다.
